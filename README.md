## 🔍 Core Visuals & Analysis Breakdown

* **Supervisor Filtering:** Dynamic slicer tracking individual managerial/sales rep performance (Aadil Khan, Aarvi Gupta, Advika Joshi, Ajay Sharma, Roshan Kumar, Vijay Singh).
* **Geographical Distribution (Map):** Pinpoints state-level market penetration across Indian territories, highlighting major hubs in Northern and Central regions.
* **Volume by Category (Bar Chart):** Evaluates high-velocity hardware:
  * *Top Volume:* Monitors (290), Mouse (236), CPUs (218), and Graphic Cards (200).
  * *Components:* SSDs (184), Keyboards (181), HDDs (168), and RAM (166).
  * *Accessories:* Cabinets, Motherboards, Computer Cases, NICs, and Printers.
* **Brand Market Share (Donut Chart):**
  * *Market Leaders:* Intel (18.04%), Samsung (16.62%), and Nvidia (14.07%).
  * *Secondary Contributors:* Dell (13.71%), Acer (7.47%), Western Digital (7.25%), Gigabyte (6.30%), Hynix (5.94%), and Seagate (4.96%).
